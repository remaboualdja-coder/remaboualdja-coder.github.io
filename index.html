01

Strong business, fair price

Shares are up 34% in a year and already sit at the consensus target (c. €20). My base-case DCF gives €21.75: only 7% upside.

02

Dupes are the underrated risk

Fragrance is 72% of revenue, and its growth slowed from +6.4% to +3.8% while US mass fragrance, driven by dupes, grew almost three times faster than prestige.

03

Upside needs Vision 2032

If management fully delivers its 2032 plan, the shares are worth about €24. If dupes keep squeezing prestige, about €17.50. That range doesn't justify a BUY today.

Part of Puig's discount is fair: family control (A/B shares), a small free float and a short listing history (IPO May 2024).

What would make me upgrade to BUY

  • Fragrance growth re-accelerating above 6%
  • Renewed takeover interest. Puig and Estée Lauder both said they hadn't ruled out future deals

What would make me downgrade to SELL

  • Prestige fragrance growth below 3%
  • A disappointing ISDIN integration (€900m cash + €300m deferred)
  • Travel retail weakening again
